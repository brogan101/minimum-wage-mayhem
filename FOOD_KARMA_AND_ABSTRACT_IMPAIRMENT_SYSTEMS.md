# Food Karma And Abstract Impairment Systems

## Food Karma Purpose

Represent petty/corner-cutting food decisions as abstract game-state choices with serious in-game consequences.

Labels:
- Revenge Meal
- Questionable Order
- Petty Sauce
- Dropped But Recoverable
- Spiritually Fresh
- Looks Fine From Here
- Food Karma Crime
- Do The Right Thing

## Food Karma Consequences

- Food Karma drops
- customer rival created
- guilt event unlocked
- bad review chance
- HR report chance
- fired risk if caught
- manager trial penalty
- redemption event unlocked

## Guilt Events

Possible guilt events:
- normal family orders
- tired nurse orders
- grandpa orders simple meal
- kid birthday meal
- polite customer thanks player
- coworker says that was messed up
- fryer whispers “bro”
- receipt printer prints “WHY”
- Nugget Tribunal dream

## Abstract Impairment Purpose

Add fictional work-readiness/energy-risk mechanics without naming real drugs or giving usage details.

Labels:
- Gas Station Focus Juice
- Break Room Fog
- Mystery Pep-Up
- Suspicious Energy
- Too Many Energy Drinks
- Shift Brain Melt
- Spiritually Clocked Out
- Fryer Tunnel Vision
- Customer-Service Astral Projection
- Not Fit For Window
- Crash Incoming
- Mop Enlightenment

## Rule

No real drugs, dosages, use instructions, or procurement. Only abstract modifiers and consequences.
