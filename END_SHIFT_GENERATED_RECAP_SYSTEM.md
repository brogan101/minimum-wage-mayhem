# End-Of-Shift Generated Recap System

The recap should be generated from actual events, not canned text.

## Include

- cash
- tips
- XP
- orders served
- accuracy
- customer Beef
- staff morale
- manager trust
- corporate approval
- promotion progress
- demotion risk
- fired risk
- suspicion
- evidence
- karma
- HR reports
- reviews
- viral clips
- missions
- side quests
- daily tasks
- most unhinged moment
- funniest object
- worst decision
- best recovery
- rumor started
- next shift warning

## Rule

The recap should pull from RestaurantMemory, EvidenceManager, StoreObjectMemory, ConsequenceMatrix, MultiKarma, and ReputationLabel managers.
