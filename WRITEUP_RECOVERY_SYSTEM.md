# Write-Up And Recovery System

## Purpose

Write-ups should be funny career pressure, not random punishment.

## Write-Up Fields

- writeup_id
- display_name
- severity
- source
- reason
- fairness
- active_duration_shifts
- promotion_penalty
- recovery_task
- can_appeal
- EventLog source
- HR text key

## Severity

1. Verbal warning
2. Written warning
3. Final warning
4. Promotion freeze
5. Demotion/firing risk

## Funny Write-Up Examples

- Handed customer a bag containing only emotional support napkins.
- Argued with receipt printer and lost.
- Used manager voice before earning manager voice.
- Failed to control sauce economy.
- Employee appeared haunted near fryer.
- Marked spill as “emotionally cleaned.”
- Customer complained burger was “too potato-forward,” somehow your fault.
- Corporate disliked your fry aura.
- Left Drive-Thru Window Warrior energy unattended.

## Recovery Options

- complete retraining
- work clean shift
- improve accuracy
- apologize to customer
- cover extra shift
- fix station issue
- coach coworker
- pass inspection
- complete performance improvement plan
- appeal to manager/corporate

## Rule

Write-ups should delay promotions but be recoverable unless severe/repeated.
