# Unhinged Incident Director Design

The UnhingedIncidentDirector sits above the regular ChaosDirector and controls rare, stupid, extreme, or chain-reaction incidents.

## Tracks

- current shift chaos level
- current Beef
- staff morale
- manager mood
- player stress
- player energy
- player reputation
- corporate pressure
- active write-ups
- demotion risk
- promotion score
- store cleanliness
- inventory loss
- shady suspicion
- customer rival count
- home-life modifiers
- recent incident types
- max allowed chaos
- recovery window status
- legendary incident chance

## Rules

- Tutorial blocks major/severe/legendary incidents.
- Recovery windows block major incidents.
- Recent incident types reduce repeat chance.
- High player struggle reduces severe incident chance.
- Strong player performance can increase curveball chance.
- Manager trial can override some gates.
- Legendary incidents are rare or mission-gated.
