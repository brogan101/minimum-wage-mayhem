# Prank Side Quest Design

Prank side quests are optional.

They can reward:
- morale
- coworker loyalty
- funny badges
- chaos reputation
- leadership score
- manager trust
- hidden story chain access

They can also punish:
- manager suspicion
- HR risk
- promotion delay
- demotion risk
- coworker rivalry
- customer confusion

## Core Side Quests

- The Good Headset Heist
- Sauce Packet Cold War
- The Fake Corporate Memo
- Break Room Trial
- The Window 3 Conspiracy
- The Mop Promotion
- The Coupon Binder Prophecy
