# Career Setback And Comeback Arcs

## Purpose

Failure should create stories and recovery goals, not just dead ends.

## Setback Sources

- bad shift performance
- angry reviews
- active write-ups
- coworker blaming player
- corporate misunderstanding
- equipment failure
- home event causing lateness
- missing shifts
- register errors
- food safety mistake
- staff morale collapse
- shady behavior noticed
- failed inspection
- bad manager evaluation
- employee snitch
- overusing savage comeback

## Possible Setbacks

- written warning
- bad performance review
- lost raise opportunity
- promotion delayed
- forced retraining
- worse shift assigned
- temporary station restriction
- coworker trust reduced
- corporate approval reduced
- customer rival created
- staff morale penalty
- demotion risk
- fired only after major/repeated failure

## Comeback Options

- work extra shift
- complete retraining
- apologize to customer
- buy staff food/perk
- fix store issue
- improve accuracy
- cover bad shift
- handle inspection
- coach coworker
- take blame
- strategically shift blame, risky
- use corporate apology
- complete performance improvement plan
- win back manager trust

## Rule

Setbacks must be recoverable unless severe and repeated.

## Example Arc: Promotion Delayed By Sauce Incident

1. Customer freaks out about sauce count.
2. Player handles poorly.
3. Bad review + write-up.
4. Promotion delayed.
5. Recovery mission: complete Sauce Precision Trial.
6. HR generates funny apology note.
7. Promotion path resumes.
