# Listen / Cause / Join / Avoid Drama System

Workplace drama should give the player personality, not force one playstyle.

## Routes

### Listen
Gain information, possibly prevent future incidents, maybe increase stress.

### Cause
Escalate chaos, shift morale, raise snitch risk, possibly create a future rival.

### Join
Pick a side, change loyalty, form rivalries, affect manager trust.

### Avoid
Keep working, maybe improve shift performance, but drama can worsen without the player.

## Validation

Each major drama chain should offer at least two routes. Important chains should offer all four.
