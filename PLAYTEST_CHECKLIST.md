# Playtest Checklist

Use this checklist for local playtests of the early build.

## Startup

- Launches without script errors.
- Main menu appears first.
- New Game starts a shift.
- Continue loads a local save or starts cleanly if no save exists.
- Controls screen is readable.
- Settings screen toggles fullscreen/windowed, accessibility, volume, and performance mode.

## In Shift

- Player can move and look around.
- Interaction prompt appears when looking at stations or items.
- Player can pick up/drop the Training Burger.
- Drive-thru handoff completes an order.
- HUD shows order/task/status information.
- Shift timer is visible.
- Objective tracker is visible.
- Station feedback is visible.
- Pause menu opens with Esc/Start.
- Resume works.
- Save/load status is clear.

## End Shift

- End Shift works from pause menu.
- Shift recap appears.
- Recap includes money, XP, customers served, accuracy, tasks, reviews, warnings, depth, and next shift recommendation.
- Return to menu works.
- Next shift can start.
- Save persists completed shift and career progress.

## Controller

- Confirm left stick movement.
- Confirm right stick look.
- Confirm A interact.
- Confirm X drop.
- Confirm Start pause.
- Record controller model tested.

## Regression Watch

- No softlock after order handoff.
- No softlock after pause/resume.
- No softlock after save/load.
- No softlock after end shift.
- No menu trapping player away from gameplay.
- Restaurant story event count remains 180.
