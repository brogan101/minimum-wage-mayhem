# Caught / Firing / Recovery System

## Purpose

Bad choices should have real consequences without making the game instantly end for every mistake.

## Caught Levels

1. Caught But Excused
2. Caught Minor
3. Caught Serious
4. Caught Major
5. Caught Legendary

## Firing Risk Factors

- major offense caught
- repeated tip jar theft
- repeated register issues
- serious food safety event caught
- customer conflict escalated badly
- abstract impairment causes major incident
- lying during HR investigation
- sabotaging coworker repeatedly
- corporate inspection failure
- store safety issue ignored
- active write-ups maxed
- manager wants scapegoat
- staff refuses to cover player

## Fired Outcomes

Avoid permanent game-over unless chosen by player.

Routes:
- restart from previous week
- demoted to Trainee
- transferred to worse location
- forced retraining arc
- probation mode
- appeal meeting mini-event
- rival restaurant job offer
- night shift punishment route
- somehow rehired route
- lost paperwork route
- accidental corporate promotion typo

## Rule

Firing should create story routes, not always delete progress.
