# Tip Jar / Register / Inventory Misconduct Systems

## Purpose

Add abstract workplace temptation systems for tips, register issues, and inventory/food cost.

## Tip Jar

The tip jar supports honest and shady choices:
- leave alone
- count honestly
- split fairly
- pocket small amount
- pocket large amount
- blame missing tips on customer
- blame New Hire
- move jar for safety
- use tip money to calm customer
- use tip money for staff snack
- catch coworker stealing tips
- stage fake sting

## Register

The register supports:
- count honestly
- report discrepancy early
- hide small discrepancy
- blame system error
- fake discount
- suspicious void
- employee discount abuse
- manager code misuse
- confess and take training

## Inventory / Food Cost

Inventory supports:
- eat fries from station
- take sauce packets home
- hide extra food in break room
- give friend free meal
- mark food as waste
- report coworker food scheme
- join coworker food scheme
- stop coworker food scheme

## Rule

These are abstract game systems. No real-world theft method should be described.
