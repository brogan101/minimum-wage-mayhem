# Prank Consequence Matrix

Every prank should calculate consequences.

## Track

- staff morale
- coworker loyalty
- coworker anger
- manager suspicion
- manager trust
- customer confusion
- Beef risk
- station delay
- cleanliness
- corporate approval
- HR risk
- promotion progress
- demotion risk
- prank war heat
- restaurant stability
- Karma impact
- rumor impact

## Example

Player hides good headset.

Result chain:
1. Coworker uses bad headset.
2. Order is misheard.
3. Customer receives wrong drink.
4. Beef increases.
5. Manager suspects player.
6. EventLog records Headset Incident.
7. Coworker starts retaliation prank next shift.

## Rule

No prank should be only a joke. It must touch at least one gameplay system.
