# Multi-Karma System

## Karma Types

- food karma
- customer karma
- coworker karma
- shady karma
- prank karma
- corporate karma
- manager karma
- chaos karma
- kindness karma
- guilt / conscience

## Purpose

Karma should influence future event selection, dialogue, reviews, coworker behavior, manager reactions, customer patience, and promotion route.

## Examples

Bad food karma:
- normal family guilt event
- Nugget Tribunal dream
- polite customer makes player feel terrible

Good coworker karma:
- staff covers for player
- coworker helps during rush
- staff warns player before inspection

High prank karma:
- coworker retaliation chance up
- prank side quests unlock
- mop mythology grows

High corporate karma:
- promotion odds improve
- staff trust may drop

High chaos karma:
- major incidents more likely
- staff may respect survival skills
