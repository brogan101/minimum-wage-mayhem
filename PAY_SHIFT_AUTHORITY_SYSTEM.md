# Pay / Shift Quality / Authority System

## Purpose

Progression should have practical benefits, not just rank names.

## Pay Rate

Track hourly wage or shift pay by rank.

Example progression:
- New Hire: terrible pay
- Trainee: tiny raise
- Crew Member: small raise
- Station roles: station bonus
- Crew Trainer: training bonus
- Shift Lead: authority raise
- Key Holder: responsibility raise
- Assistant Manager: salary-like pay
- Store Manager: better pay + performance bonus

## Shift Quality

Early ranks get worse shifts:
- bad opening
- closing shift
- understaffed shift
- late-night chaos
- weekend rush

Higher ranks gain influence:
- request better shifts
- pick shift focus
- assign coworkers
- avoid some awful shifts
- schedule self
- build backup coverage

## Authority Unlocks

Authority increases over time:
- New Hire: can only do tasks
- Crew Member: can recover small mistakes
- Crew Trainer: can train others
- Shift Lead: can assign stations/breaks
- Key Holder: can open/close
- Assistant Manager: can schedule/discipline
- Store Manager: can hire/fire/upgrade/plan

## Validation

Promotions must unlock at least one practical change:
- pay
- shift type
- area
- station
- authority
- tool
- responsibility
- chaos type
